#Reasoning
Method, assumptions & sources

A common spending denominator

Tasks / subscription $ = multiplier ÷ AA cost / task
Tasks / $20 = 20 × rounded tasks / subscription $

Tasks per subscription dollar are rounded to one decimal before multiplying by 20. A budget scenario scales the resulting values by budget / 20.

Sonnet Medium (intelligence 41) is below the qualifying floor. Grok Medium lacks the required benchmark values. Neither appears in this dataset; filters cannot add missing configurations.

What counts as Pareto optimal?

No other included configuration offers at least as much intelligence and throughput, with a strict improvement in either dimension. The explorer recomputes the frontier among the configurations matching your search, subscription, and floor filters.

A snapshot, not a usage guarantee

The Claude and ChatGPT multipliers are estimates derived from SemiAnalysis's API-equivalent subscription-value analysis. Cursor's multiplier is calculated from the author's usage exports: metered API-equivalent usage divided by subscription spend.

The Cursor exports included both Grok and Claude usage. Applying the full 20.7× multiplier to Grok assumes both available usage pools can be allocated to it. Benchmark tasks are a spending proxy; actual task volume depends on the workload and usage limits. Figures reflect the 8 October 2026 snapshot.
